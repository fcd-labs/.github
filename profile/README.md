# Full Circle Delivery

**AI needs delivery. Delivery needs AI.**

Full Circle Delivery (FCD) is een model om software te leveren dat de hele cirkel rond gaat: van intentie via delivery en product naar leren. Meer op de site: https://fcd-labs.github.io/fcd/

## Showcases

Drie showcases met hetzelfde product, ShowKitchen, op drie manieren van werken. De deelproducten van ShowKitchen hebben elk één repository, die alle showcases gebruiken.

| Showcase | Way of working | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Demo](https://fcd-labs.github.io/fcd/showcases/demo/) | [fcd-demo](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd-demo) | werkend |
| [Classic](https://fcd-labs.github.io/fcd/showcases/classic/) | [fcd-classic](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd-classic) | placeholder |
| [Ideal](https://fcd-labs.github.io/fcd/showcases/ideal/) | [fcd-ideal](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd-ideal) | placeholder |

|---|---|
| Bestellen | [sk-bestellen](https://github.com/fcd-labs/sk-bestellen) |

Het gereedschap van FCD, voor alle showcases: [fcd-pipeline](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd-pipeline) en [fcd-dashboard](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd-dashboard).

Het model, de declaratieve bestanden en de site: [fcd](https://github.com/fcd-labs/fcd).
